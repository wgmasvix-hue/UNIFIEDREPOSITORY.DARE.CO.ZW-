# DARE AI Product

Product surface of DARE_AI_ARCHITECTURE.md. Positioning: grounded source
intelligence — never a generic ChatGPT clone.

## 1. Primary experiences

**ASK DARE** — questions answered from DARE sources, every factual answer
cited. **READ** — open the original. **STUDY** — summaries, concepts,
definitions, questions, flashcards, guides, quizzes, discussion prompts.
**ANALYSE** — dataset schema, variables, missingness, geographic/temporal
coverage, descriptives, AI-readiness, research questions (never fabricated).
**DISCOVER / RESEARCH** — graph-powered exploration (see KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md).

## 2. Proof questions (must-answer set)

"What research exists about maize post-harvest losses in Zimbabwe?" · "What
datasets exist about maternal health in Africa?" · "Which institutions
research climate change in Southern Africa?" · "What datasets can I use to
study African food systems?" · "Which African researchers work on food
safety?" Each combines repository resources + harvested externals +
intelligence + graph + AI.

## 3. Language strategy (infrastructure, not claims)

Pipeline order: language detection → multilingual metadata → multilingual
search → translated summaries, with source-language preservation and room
for future African-language models. Practical support first (Shona content
already exists in `dare-datasets`/MasakhaNER seeds). State of support must
always be honest in-product: nothing is claimed until it ships.

## 4. Learning integration

Learn consumes DARE knowledge through controlled APIs instead of a
parallel universe; Cheryl stays curriculum-aware, Garikai tertiary — both
as personas over approved resources. Study/quiz generation already exists
in Learn; the platform move is sourcing it from DARE evidence with
citations.
